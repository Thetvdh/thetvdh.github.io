I am a Cyber Security Consultant currently working for UK Based Cyber Security consultantcy. I currently hold the Cyber Scheme Team Member (CSTM) Certification along with Burp Suite Certified Practitioner (BSCP). I specialise in Web Application and Mobile Application penetration testing for both iOS and Android. 

I have recently been moving into the vulnerability research sector with an increased focus on niche areas of research such as browser extension reverse engineering and IoT device analysis. 

This site will be a repository of things I have researched and found interesting, along with some of my older HackTheBox and TryHackMe writeups that I wrote while I was learning pentesting.
